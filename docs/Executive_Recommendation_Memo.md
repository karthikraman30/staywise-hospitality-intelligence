# Executive Recommendation Memo

## Business Question

How can StayWise recover avoidable revenue leakage from cancellations, underutilized inventory, and unprofitable channel mix?

## Executive Summary

The analysis found **$13,659,551** in cancellation-linked revenue leakage across **119,390** bookings after applying deposit-based cancellation fee recovery. The current cancellation rate is **37.0%**, retained cancellation fee revenue is **$3,067,686**, and estimated net revenue after channel commission is **$24,677,692**.

The highest-priority opportunity is **Resort Hotel | Online TA | TA/TO | 181+ days**, with an estimated **$153,351** recoverable revenue opportunity.

## Recommended Action

Prioritize: **Pilot staged deposit or reconfirmation workflow for high-lead OTA bookings**.

This intervention is recommended because the segment combines high booking volume, high cancellation exposure, and a meaningful recoverable revenue estimate. The strongest modeled cancellation-risk driver is **Deposit type**, which supports targeting the intervention through booking behavior rather than applying a generic policy to all customers.

## Decision Logic

- Revenue leakage was defined as canceled booking value less assumed cancellation fee recovery by deposit type.
- Net revenue was defined as realized revenue less estimated channel commission.
- Recoverable revenue was estimated from segment-level lost revenue and achievable cancellation-rate reduction.
- Recommendations were ranked by recoverable revenue, cancellation risk, and booking volume.

## KPI Monitoring After Implementation

- Cancellation rate by lead-time band and channel
- Realized revenue and lost revenue
- Net RevPAR
- Direct share of bookings
- Recovery against estimated opportunity

## Caveats

The hotel booking data is public and historical. Channel commission, inventory, targets, events, and intervention metadata are synthetic business context tables created for portfolio demonstration. They should be treated as realistic assumptions, not actual company records.
